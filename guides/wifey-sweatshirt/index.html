The wifey sweatshirt: why every bride wants one and how to pick it

Updated 2026-10-23 · Brides & Wifey designs · gift guide

Why a wifey sweatshirt is the bride uniform you didn&#x27;t know you needed

A wifey sweatshirt is exactly what it sounds like: a cozy crewneck or pullover with some variation of "wifey," "mrs," or bride-themed text that announces your newly married (or about to be married) status to the world. It's become the unofficial uniform for getting-ready mornings, bachelorette weekends, honeymoon flights, and those first few months when you're still giddy about checking the "married" box on forms.

The appeal is simple. You get something comfortable to actually wear during all those pre-wedding events where a white dress feels like overkill, plus a keepsake that reminds you of this specific moment in your life. Unlike a sash or tiara you'll wear once and stuff in a drawer, a good sweatshirt becomes part of your regular rotation, especially if you choose wisely.

The getting-ready morning sweatshirt

This is the sweatshirt you wear while your hair is in rollers and someone's airbrushing foundation on your face. It needs to pull over your head easily without messing up any prep work, which means you want a relaxed fit with a wide neck opening.

Look for classic text-based designs: "Bride," "Mrs. [Your New Last Name]," or "Wifey" in simple fonts. Script fonts photograph beautifully but avoid anything too ornate that becomes unreadable in photos. Neutral colors work best here because they won't cast weird color reflections on your face in getting-ready photos. Think cream, soft pink, light gray, or white.

Price range: $25-45 for a decent quality crewneck that won't pill after one wash.

Bachelorette weekend sweatshirts

The bachelorette sweatshirt can be cheekier than your wedding day one. This is where "Wifey" with a little attitude fits perfectly, or phrases that reference your last hurrah, the bride tribe, or inside jokes with your group.

Consider ordering matching or coordinating sweatshirts for your whole bridal party. Same color in different sizes works well, or the bride gets one color while bridesmaids get another. Just make sure you order early and confirm sizing with everyone because returns get complicated with custom orders.

For destination bachelorettes, a sweatshirt is actually practical. Airplanes are freezing, beach nights get chilly, and you'll want something to throw on over your outfit between bars. Choose mid-weight fleece that packs relatively flat.

Price range: $20-35 per sweatshirt when ordering multiples for a group.

Honeymoon and travel sweatshirts

A bride sweatshirt or wifey shirt makes airport outfit sense. You're comfortable for a long flight, and it's a subtle way to signal newlywed status that sometimes scores you free champagne or upgrades (no promises, but it happens).

For honeymoon wear, skip anything too bridal-specific like "Bride" in huge letters. "Mrs." or "Wifey" with smaller, subtler text feels less costume-y when you're wearing it to breakfast in Cabo. You want something that reads as a regular sweatshirt from ten feet away.

Dark colors hide travel grime better than white. Navy, black, forest green, or burgundy all work. Make sure it's a fabric blend that won't wrinkle into oblivion in your suitcase.

Price range: $30-50 for something you'll rewear on future trips.

Sizing: bigger is better (usually)

Sweatshirts are meant to be cozy, and most people size up for that oversized, borrowed-from-your-husband look. If you normally wear a medium, a large will give you that relaxed fit that looks good in photos and feels good on the couch.

That said, check the specific measurements. A Gildan 18000 crewneck, for example, runs pretty true to size with a unisex fit, so sizing up one size gives you room without drowning in fabric. If you're petite, your true size might actually work better, or you'll be swimming in sleeve length.

For getting-ready robes situations where you're pulling it over styled hair, you definitely want that larger neck opening that comes with sizing up. Test by pulling it on and off over your head a few times if possible.

When to choose a bride sweatsuit instead

A matching bride sweatsuit (sweatshirt plus sweatpants or joggers) is peak comfort for long getting-ready mornings, recovery brunches, or the entire week after your wedding when you're exhausted and don't want real pants.

The trick is making sure the pieces actually match in color and fabric weight. Buying them as a set from the same seller solves this. Mixing and matching from different brands usually results in slightly different shades that look mismatched in photos.

Sweatsuits photograph well when everyone's wearing them (bridal party in matching sets) or when you style them intentionally with slippers and a messy bun. They photograph less well when you're the only one in a sweatsuit in a room of people in real clothes, so think about context.

Price range: $50-80 for a matching set.

Generic "bride tribe" stuff is fine, but a sweatshirt referencing your actual personality, your relationship, or an inside joke will get way more wear. What to skip when shopping for a wifey sweatshirt

Avoid sweatshirts with tons of embellishments that make them uncomfortable or hand-wash only. Sequins, rhinestones, and puffy paint all seem cute until you're trying to pack one in a suitcase or wash it after someone spilled mimosa on it.

Skip anything so trendy it'll look dated in photos in five years. Super specific 2020s slang or meme references might not age well. Classic text in a nice font is boring but timeless.

Don't buy a sweatshirt in a size you hope to be. Buy the size you are right now, or even size up. Wedding stress is real, and the last thing you need is a sweatshirt that doesn't fit adding to it.

Finally, avoid buying too far in advance. If your wedding is a year away, wait until closer to order custom items. Your taste might change, or you might gain or lose weight, or the sweatshirt might sit in your closet so long it doesn't feel special anymore when you finally wear it.

Questions people ask

When should I wear my wifey sweatshirt?

Wear it for getting-ready photos on your wedding morning, during bachelorette weekends, on your honeymoon travel days, or anytime during your engagement and newlywed phase when you want to be cozy and a little celebratory. Many brides keep wearing them as regular sweatshirts long after the wedding.

Should I size up or down for a bride sweatshirt?

Most people size up one size for that cozy, oversized look that photographs well and feels comfortable. Check the specific brand's measurements, but when in doubt, bigger is usually better for sweatshirts.

What's the difference between a wifey sweatshirt and a bride sweatshirt?

They're essentially the same thing with different text. "Bride" sweatshirts work for engagement through wedding day, while "Wifey" or "Mrs." sweatshirts technically work after you're married, though most people use the terms interchangeably during the whole wedding season.
