Chucklify app for our own business account. Last updated 13 October 2026.

What this connection does

Chucklify publishes pins for our own product designs to our own Pinterest business account (@chucklify). Each pin links to a product page on chucklify.com, where the shirt, sweatshirt or mug can be bought on Etsy. The app also reads our own pin and board statistics (impressions, saves, clicks) to produce a weekly report.

The app is for one account only. It does not serve other Pinterest users and does not post on anyone else's behalf.

What we request

  • boards:read — list our boards so each pin goes to the right board.
  • pins:read — read analytics for our own pins.
  • pins:write and boards:write — create our own pins on our own boards (Pinterest requires both for pin creation).
  • user_accounts:read — confirm the connected account is @chucklify.

How the connection works (OAuth 2.0)

  1. Click Connect Pinterest below. You are sent to Pinterest's own sign-in and consent screen.
  2. Sign in with the Pinterest account you want to connect and click Allow. We never see your password.
  3. Pinterest redirects back to chucklify.com/oauth/callback/ with a one-time authorization code.
  4. The code is exchanged for an access token and a refresh token, which are stored as encrypted secrets used only by our publishing job. Tokens refresh automatically.

You can revoke the connection at any time in Pinterest under Settings → Apps. Doing so stops all automated pinning immediately.
