How to pick the right one

Match the item to how they'll use it: a tee for everyday, a crewneck sweatshirt for autumn and winter (the most-gifted item), a mug when you don't know their size. All three carry the same design, so a matching set for two people is easy.

Sizing and colours

Tees are Comfort Colors 1717 in a relaxed unisex fit, S–2XL, in eleven garment-dyed colours. Sweatshirts are Gildan 18000, also unisex S–2XL. When in doubt, order their usual size; the fit is relaxed, not oversized. See the size guide for measurements.

Timing

Everything is printed to order in the USA: 2–5 business days to make, 3–8 business days to deliver in the US with tracking. For December gifts, order by the second week of December; for Halloween, by mid-October.

Questions

What are good gifts for dog moms and dog dads under $30?

A Comfort Colors tee or an 11 oz mug from any design here is under $30 at full price, and under $25 during shop sales. The crewneck sweatshirt is the step-up gift.

Can I get a matching set?

Yes — every design is available as a tee, sweatshirt and mug, so two people can match without getting the same thing.

Where are the shirts printed and shipped from?

Every item is printed to order in the USA by our print partner and shipped from the USA with tracking. Production takes 2–5 business days; US delivery is usually 3–8 business days after that.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes — free standard shipping on US orders of $35 or more through our Etsy shop. Smaller orders pay standard Etsy shipping at checkout.
