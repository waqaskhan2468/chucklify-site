Cat shirts for people who are owned by their cat

Cat humour is dry and a little superior, and so are these. Black cat designs lead in autumn — spooky season is a cat-person holiday — and the rest of the year it's the cat who stole your social life, judged you first and runs the house. Retro type, a cat silhouette that reads from across the room, printed to order.

Which one

The Comfort Colors tee is the everyday choice and comes in Pepper and Black for the full black-cat effect. The crewneck is the cozy-season gift. The mug is for the person whose cat sits on their keyboard.

Questions people ask

What is the best cat mom shirt to give as a gift?

The one that matches the person, not the category. Our cat people designs are written about specific moments (owned by the cat), so pick the line that sounds like them. The crewneck sweatshirt is the most-gifted item; the mug is the safe choice when you don't know their size.

Are these cat mom shirts unisex?

Yes. Tees are Comfort Colors 1717 and sweatshirts are Gildan 18000, both relaxed unisex fits in S–2XL. See the size guide for chest and length measurements.

Where are the shirts printed and shipped from?

Every item is printed to order in the USA by our print partner and shipped from the USA with tracking. Production takes 2–5 business days; US delivery is usually 3–8 business days after that.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes — free standard shipping on US orders of $35 or more through our Etsy shop. Smaller orders pay standard Etsy shipping at checkout.

What is the return policy?

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 30 days through Etsy. If anything arrives damaged, misprinted or wrong, message us with a photo and we reprint or refund straight away — no return needed.
