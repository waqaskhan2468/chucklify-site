Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Chucklify ("we", "us") sells printed apparel and gifts through our Etsy shop and promotes them on Pinterest and other platforms. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Orders. Purchases are made on Etsy. Etsy and our print-on-demand partner (Printify and its print providers) receive the name, shipping address and order details needed to produce and deliver your item. We see the same order information inside those platforms. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Website visits. chucklify.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. Our host may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited to keep the site secure. We may use the Pinterest tag or similar analytics to measure which pins lead to visits; these use cookies and you can block them in your browser.
  • Messages. If you contact us through Etsy or email, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

How we use information

  • To produce, ship and support your order.
  • To understand which designs and pins people like, so we make better ones.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as tax and marketplace rules.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.

Pinterest and other platforms

We use Pinterest's API only to publish our own pins and to read analytics for our own account. We do not access other users' Pinterest data. Your use of Pinterest, Etsy, Printify or GitHub is governed by their own privacy policies.

Cookies

chucklify.com sets no first-party cookies of its own. Third-party analytics or conversion tags, where used, may set cookies; you can disable them in your browser settings.

Data retention

Order records are kept for as long as Etsy and tax law require. Support messages are deleted when no longer needed. Analytics data is aggregated and not tied to you personally.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, or to object to certain processing. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. For order data held by Etsy or Printify, you can also contact them directly.

Children

Our products are sold to adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the effective date above.
